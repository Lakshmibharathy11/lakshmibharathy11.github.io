Marketing mix model and budget allocator

Bayesian MMM built with Google Meridian on its simulated demo dataset: 156 weeks of data from 40 markets across 5 paid channels, modeled at the national level. VIF-informed priors handle collinearity between channels, and a constrained optimizer recommends how to reallocate a fixed $219.5M budget. The model converged across 4 MCMC chains (max R-hat 1.004) and predicts weekly revenue within 4.4% (MAPE). Results are delivered through a five-page Looker Studio dashboard.

Moving $4.8M into the most underfunded channel ($7.7M shifted in total) projects +0.35% (about $1.0M) more media-driven revenue at the same budget; every channel's marginal ROI is below 1.0, so reallocation beats adding spend
Python, Google Meridian, Bayesian inference, Looker Studio

Graduate thesis

Eye-Tracking Based Direction Control Using Machine Learning Technique
M.S. thesis, San José State University, May 2026

Eye-tracking lets a machine take direction from where a person looks, which helps when their hands aren't a reliable input. Gaze data is a noisy biological signal, full of micro-movements, blinks, and person-to-person differences, so the core problem was finding a representation stable enough to classify reliably, both for one person and across people.

  1. Data collectionLab-collected eye-tracking recordings of directional gaze
  2. ValidationChecked labels against physical gaze behavior and for directional bias
  3. Feature engineeringCoordinate, time-aggregated, and eye-image representations
  4. ModelingClassical ML classifiers compared with fine-tuned CNNs
  5. EvaluationPersonalized and cross-user generalization testing
