01Marketing Measurement

Bayesian Marketing Mix Model & Budget Optimizer

Which channels actually drive revenue, and how should a fixed budget be split? A Bayesian MMM built with Google Meridian on its simulated demo dataset: 156 weeks, 40 markets, 5 paid channels, modeled at the national level.

  • Handled channel collinearity with VIF-informed priors so spend effects weren't confused with each other.
  • Validated the model: converged across 4 MCMC chains (max R-hat 1.004) and predicts weekly revenue within 4.4% MAPE.
  • Recommended a reallocation with a constrained optimizer: shift $7.7M in total, including $4.8M into the most underfunded channel, at the same $219.5M budget.
  • Delivered results through a five-page Looker Studio dashboard for non-technical stakeholders.

02Causal Inference · Incrementality

Causal Uplift & Incrementality Platform

Which users convert because of an ad, versus users who would have converted anyway? Uplift models on the Criteo dataset (13.9M users) to stop spending on people who don't need persuading.

  • Compared three uplift approaches (T-learner, X-learner, transformed outcome) and selected transformed outcome on Qini score (0.726), evaluated with Qini curves and AUUC.
  • Segmented the audience and found 13.2% of users are "persuadables," the only group where ads increase conversions.
  • Deployed a decision tool: Streamlit dashboard on AWS EC2 with outputs in S3, plus an LLM budget advisor whose numeric claims are checked against model outputs.

03Customer Analytics · Lifecycle

Customer Retention Priority Engine: RFM, CLV & Churn

Which customers are worth the retention budget? RFM segmentation, customer lifetime value, and churn risk for about 5,878 customers from the Online Retail II dataset, combined into one priority view.

  • Caught and fixed data leakage: Recency was leaking the churn label, so the first model looked better than it really was. Removed it before finalizing.
  • Chose the simpler model on evidence: logistic regression reached 69.6% accuracy after the fix; Random Forest was tested and underperformed.
  • Ranked customers by value at risk so retention effort goes to the accounts that matter most.

04Hackathon FinalistDecision Automation · Agentic AI

Weekly Business Review (WBR) AI Agent

Finalist, AWS Community Day Bay Area Agentic AI Hackathon. One of 5 teams (of 12) selected to present live at the AWS Builder Loft, San Francisco, Oct 2026.

Every week analysts spend hours explaining why a metric moved. This agent does the first pass: it investigates weekly e-commerce metrics (Olist dataset), flags unusual changes, and writes a review memo, with a second agent checking each claim before it reaches a reader.

  • Designed a two-agent system: an analyst agent investigates metric changes through query tools, and a verifier agent checks its claims for hallucination.
  • Built anomaly detection using robust z-scores, with low-confidence rules for small samples so noise isn't reported as a trend.
  • Safe by design: read-only guardrails, so the agents cannot modify tables or reach other AWS resources.
  • Proved the verifier works live: ran on AWS (Bedrock Nova Micro + Athena) at the finals; on a run with wrong-week evidence, the verifier rejected the report and withheld all 6 claims.
  • My role: idea and submission lead; owned the data and query layer (S3, metric queries), the tool functions, the analyst agent loop, and anomaly detection.

M.S. Thesis · SJSU ScholarWorks

Eye-Tracking Based Direction Control Using Machine Learning Technique

M.S. thesis, San José State University, May 2026

Eye-tracking lets a machine take direction from where a person looks, which helps when their hands aren't a reliable input. Gaze is a noisy biological signal, full of micro-movements, blinks, and person-to-person differences. The core problem was finding a representation stable enough to classify reliably, both for one person and across people.

Personal assistive devicesWheelchair navigation for one repeat user, where the model can be calibrated to that individual.
Shared control systemsTeleoperated drones or robots handed between operators, where the model must work for someone new.
